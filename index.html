The Science of Week 3–8 Shedding

1. Why are you seeing hairs fall out now?
Topical Minoxidil stimulates micro-circulation, pushing stagnant resting-phase (telogen) hairs out of their follicles to make way for active-growth roots.

2. Is this permanent hair loss?
No. The hairs coming out are older, thinner strands. Underneath, a healthier, thicker strand is already taking root.

3. The Number 1 Mistake Men Make:
Quitting during weeks 3 to 8 is the primary reason men think Minoxidil "didn't work." Staying consistent through this period yields visible thickening by month 3 to 4.
